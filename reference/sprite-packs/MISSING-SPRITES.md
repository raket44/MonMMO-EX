# Battle sprites still missing from the Gen 5-style packs

Regenerated by `./gradlew :launcher:stageExpansionClientContent` as launcher/build/expansion-client/sprite-pack-missing.csv; this copy was taken 2026-09-03.

Pack file names: `<dex>.png` front, `<dex>b.png` back, add `s` for shiny (`652b.png`, `652sb.png`); forms are `<dex>_N` in the games' form order, Gigantamax `<dex>_g`, regional forms in their own folder. 96x96, indexed PNG, transparent background.

Forms of Gen 1-5 species (Unown letters, Rotom appliances, Arceus types...) are left out: the client draws those natively.
